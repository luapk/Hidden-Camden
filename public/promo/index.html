The most musical half-mile on Earth.

A groundbreaking rock 'n' stroll tour through Camden's music venues, told by the people who made it. Walk the route, stories unlock the moment your feet arrive, and two free Wolfie's land in your phone along the way.

The 60-second film

How it works

Walk up. Story unlocks. Drinks on us.

01

Walk to the venue

Eleven stops from Camden Town tube to the Roundhouse, and the whole walk is free. Each stop stays locked until you are physically standing in front of it, then it opens.

02

The story unlocks

Your phone buzzes and a two-minute story plays, from your host or the legend who owns that stop. The witch, the boxer, the lie about jazz, the kebab shop's back room.

03

Two Wolfie's on us

Hear the third story and your first Wolfie's lands in your wallet. Make it to the Roundhouse finale and so does the second. They keep for a week, so there is no rush.

Giving back

A share of every tour goes to the Roundhouse.

Camden made the music. The next generation should get the same shot. A portion of every tour supports the Roundhouse's work getting young people from the borough into music, radio and the arts, in the room that started it all up the road.
